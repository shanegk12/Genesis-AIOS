# About Genesis K-12 Academy

Genesis K-12 Academy is a hands-on, faith-based engineering education company for homeschool families. It offers 18-week courses — not box subscriptions — built around two tracks: Creationeering (an engineering + business paradigm that draws on intelligent design) and Build courses (project-based construction and making). The company is structured to eventually serve four age bands: K-2nd, 3rd-5th, 6th-8th, and 9th-12th.

The current product in development is a middle school Creationeering course plus a mousetrap build course. A beta of the first 3 weeks shipped in May 2026. Courses are delivered via LearnWorlds. The company is pre-revenue, funded by an angel investor, with a launch event planned for July 2026 in Tennessee. Payment infrastructure (LearnWorlds storefront + QuickBooks) is being set up ahead of launch. Most inbound leads come through referrals from Dr. Mark Horstemeyer, a mentor and close collaborator.
