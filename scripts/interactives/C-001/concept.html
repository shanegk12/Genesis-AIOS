What is Creationeering

Concept Overview

What is Engineering?

Engineering is like being a super-solver! It's all about using math and science to invent and build things that fix real-world problems. Think about bridges, phones, or even the systems that deliver water to your house – engineers designed all of them!

Creationeering: Our Special Approach

At Genesis K-12 Academy, we don't just learn engineering; we learn 'Creationeering™'. This means we combine science, engineering, and even starting new projects (entrepreneurship) with our faith. It helps us understand how God created the world and how we can use our gifts to make it even better.

God, Creativity, and You

The Bible tells us that God created everything out of nothing, bringing order and beauty. He made us in His image, which means we also have the amazing ability to think, create, and innovate. When we use our creativity to solve problems and build new things, we are honoring God and continuing His work of creation.

Exploring Different Engineering Paths

Just like there are many different ways to help people, there are many types of engineering! We'll explore fields like Industrial and Systems, Mechanical, Computer, and Electrical engineering. Each one uses unique skills to create solutions and contribute to God's kingdom in different ways.

Key Takeaway

Creationeering™ teaches us that engineering is a powerful way to use our God-given talents in science, math, and creativity to solve problems, innovate, and contribute to the world, all while honoring our Creator.
