Entrepreneurship

Concept Overview

Building a Business God's Way: Pray, Think, Create

This lesson introduces us to entrepreneurship, which is all about starting and running your own business. We learn how to do this by following a special God-centered approach called 'Creationeering™': Pray, Think, Create, and always Give God the Glory. This helps us find peace and guidance, even when things get tough, just like Isaiah 26:3-4 teaches us.

What Makes an Entrepreneur?

An entrepreneur is someone who starts a business, often taking financial risks for the chance of big rewards. Successful entrepreneurs are brave, confident, and have a clear vision for what they want to achieve. They are also strong leaders who can inspire others to believe in their business idea and work together for success.

Understanding Your Business: Risks, Rewards, and Responsibilities

Starting a business comes with both risks and rewards. We'll learn how to understand these challenges and how to reduce risks through faith and careful research (mitigation). We'll also explore important business areas like Human Resources (taking care of employees) and how to manage resources responsibly, which is called stewardship.

Key Takeaway

Entrepreneurship is an exciting journey of creating and managing a business, guided by God's principles. By praying, thinking, creating, and giving God the glory, we can develop leadership skills, manage risks, and build businesses that serve our community while honoring Him.
