📦 Components

Chassis / Frame
The rigid body structure. Geometry determines strength. F=ma: less mass = more acceleration.
Rear Axle
Transfers rotational force to wheels. Torque = Force × radius (algebra).
Drive Wheels
3rd Law: Wheels push back on ground; ground pushes car forward (action-reaction).
Mousetrap Spring
Stores potential energy. 1st Law: releases force to overcome inertia and start motion.
Lever Arm
Extended arm multiplies torque. Geometry: longer arm = greater mechanical advantage.

⚖️ Newton's Laws

1st Law — Inertia
Object at rest stays at rest until the spring releases force.
2nd Law — F = ma
Lighter chassis + more spring force = greater acceleration.
3rd Law — Action-Reaction
Wheels push ground back → ground propels car forward.
