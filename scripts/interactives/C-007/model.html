OCV Legend

Objective — What you want to accomplish
Constraint — A limit or rule you must work within
Variable — Something that can change & affect outcome

Problem Statement

The car engine fails to maintain safe operating temperature, causing component wear.

Objective + Constraints + Variables + 5W

System Components

  • ■ Objective
    Engine Block
    The core structure housing pistons & cylinders. Goal: maintain efficient combustion.
  • ■ Constraint
    Oil Pan
    Holds engine oil. Constraint: oil level must stay within min/max marks.
  • ■ Variable
    Oil Pump
    Circulates oil through engine. Variable: pump pressure changes with RPM.
  • ■ Variable
    Coolant System
    Transfers heat away. Variable: coolant temp rises under heavy engine load.
  • ■ Constraint
    Thermostat
    Regulates coolant flow. Constraint: must open at exact rated temperature.

Engine Block

The engine block is the heart of the system. The engineer's objective is to keep it running efficiently. Notice how all other components either support this goal (objectives), limit what we can do (constraints), or change based on conditions (variables).
