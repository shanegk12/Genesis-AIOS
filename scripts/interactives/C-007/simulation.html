🛠️ The OCV Method

Engineers don't jump straight to solutions — they define the problem first. The OCV method helps you break any challenge into three parts:

O — Objective C — Constraint V — Variable
  • Objective: What you want to accomplish.
  • Constraint: A limit or rule you must work within.
  • Variable: Something that can change and affect the outcome.

In this activity you will read 3 real-world scenarios and sort each item into the correct OCV category. You'll also answer questions about problem definition. Good luck!
