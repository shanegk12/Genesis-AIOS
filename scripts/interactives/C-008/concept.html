Ethics in Engineering

Concept Overview

What is Creationeering?

Creationeering is a special way of thinking about engineering that combines building and designing with taking good care of God's creation. It reminds us that as engineers, we have a big responsibility to honor and protect the world around us, just as God intended. This means making choices that are not only smart and effective but also good for people and the planet.

Why Do Ethics Matter in Engineering?

Ethics are like a moral compass that guides our decisions, especially when those decisions affect others. As engineers, you'll design things that impact people's safety, health, and daily lives, like bridges or new technologies. Thinking ethically means asking if your designs are safe, fair, and beneficial for everyone, considering things like hazardous materials and how easy something is to use.

The Engineer's Rulebook: NSPE Code of Ethics

Just like a game has rules, engineers have a 'rulebook' called the NSPE Code of Ethics. This code helps engineers make good choices and act responsibly in their jobs. It's a guide to ensure that all engineers prioritize public safety and well-being in everything they create and build.

Engineers as Stewards: Being 'Salt and Light'

The Bible teaches us about stewardship, which means taking care of the resources God has given us, including the Earth and its people. As Creationeers, you have a unique role to be 'salt and light' in the world through your engineering work. This means using your skills to make positive changes, solve problems, and show God's love and care through your designs and actions.

Key Takeaway

Ethics in engineering isn't just about following rules; it's about making thoughtful choices that honor God's creation and serve others. By embracing Creationeering, you learn to design with purpose, prioritizing safety, community well-being, and responsible stewardship in every project.
