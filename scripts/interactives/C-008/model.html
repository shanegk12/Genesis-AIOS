⚙ Components
Design Core
The central planning unit where all engineering decisions originate. Represents the engineer's intentional design process.
⚖ NSPE: Engineers must hold public safety paramount in all design decisions.
Safety Layer
Protective mechanisms that ensure hazardous materials and structural risks are contained and controlled.
⚖ Creationeering: Stewardship means protecting people and creation from harm.
Community Interface
The point where engineering systems interact with users and the broader community. User experience lives here.
⚖ Salt & Light: Engineers serve as light in their communities through ethical practice.
Stewardship Ring
Environmental and resource management layer. Ensures the design honors God's creation through responsible material use.
⚖ Dominion: First calling of mankind — to steward and care for creation.
Ethics Framework
The overarching NSPE Code of Ethics that governs all professional engineering decisions and behaviors.
⚖ Free Will: God designed choice into creation — engineers choose ethical paths.

📖 Key Vocabulary

Creationeering: Engineering + ethical stewardship of God's creation.

NSPE Code: Rulebook for professional engineering ethics.

Stewardship: Duty to protect and manage creation.

🎯 Learning Goal

Connect engineering decisions to Biblical principles of stewardship, safety, and being "salt and light" in your community.
