📋 NSPE Code of Ethics — Key Principles

  • Engineers must hold public safety, health, and welfare above all else.
  • Engineers must be honest and truthful in all professional communications.
  • Engineers must act as faithful agents for clients and employers — within ethical limits.
  • Engineers must avoid deception and conflicts of interest.
  • Engineers are responsible for the environmental impact of their work (stewardship).
