Key Vocabulary

✓
Analysis
The process of breaking down a complex system or idea into its smaller, simpler parts to understand how it works.
✓
Synthesis
The process of combining separate elements or ideas to form a coherent whole, often leading to a new understanding, design, or solution.
✓
Creationeering™
A framework that integrates engineering principles with a biblical worldview, guiding the design and development of solutions for God's creation.
✓
Multiscale Modeling
A scientific approach that connects observations and phenomena at different size or time scales, from atomic to macroscopic.
✓
Objective
The specific goal or purpose that a design or solution aims to achieve.
✓
Constraint
A limitation or restriction that must be considered during the design process, such as budget, time, materials, or safety regulations.
✓
Variable
A factor or element in a design that can be changed or adjusted to achieve the objective within the given constraints.
✓
Optimization
The process of finding the best possible solution or design by adjusting variables to meet objectives while satisfying constraints.
✓
System
A set of interacting or interdependent components forming an integrated whole, working together for a common purpose.
✓
Emergent Property
A characteristic or behavior of a whole system that is not present in its individual parts and arises only when the parts interact.
