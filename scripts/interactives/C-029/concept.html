Protecting Your Business

Concept Overview

Beyond the Patent: Protecting Your Whole Business

Last time, we learned about Patents, which protect your cool new inventions. But a business is more than just one invention! This lesson will show you how to protect your entire company, its name, and all the important information you create.

Trademarks: Your Business's Promise

Think of a Trademark as your company's unique fingerprint. It's a logo, name, or symbol that tells customers who you are and that they can trust your product. Trademarks help people know they're getting the real deal and not a fake.

Copyrights: Protecting Your Creative Information

Engineers don't just build things; they also create tons of important information! Copyrights protect your original writings, drawings, computer code, and even your lesson plans. This means others can't just copy and use your hard work without permission.

Stewardship: Protecting God's Gifts

In Creationeering, we believe God has given us amazing abilities to create. Protecting our inventions, brands, and information is part of being good stewards of these gifts. It means we use wisdom and fairness in how we create and share our work, just as Proverbs 11:1 teaches us about honest weights and measures.

Key Takeaway

Protecting your business means using Patents for inventions, Trademarks for your brand, and Copyrights for your creative information. This is all part of being a good steward of the amazing things God helps us create, ensuring fairness and quality in all we do.
