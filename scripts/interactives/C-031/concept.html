What is Procurement?

Concept Overview

Getting What You Need (Wisely!)

Procurement is like being a super-smart shopper for your engineering projects. It's all about making sure you get the right parts, materials, and even help from others to build your amazing creations. Just like a chef carefully picks fresh ingredients, you'll learn how to choose the best items for your designs.

The Procurement Journey: From Idea to Item

Imagine you need a specific robot arm. The procurement journey starts with figuring out exactly what you need, then finding a good supplier, asking for prices (a quotation!), and finally placing an order (a purchase order). We'll explore each step to make sure your items arrive just right.

Choosing Wisely: Quality, Cost, and Time

When you're procuring, you'll need to think about three big things: how good is it (quality), how much does it cost, and when will it arrive (timeliness). We'll use a cool tool called OCV (Objective, Constraint, Variable) to help you make the best choices, balancing all these factors like a pro.

Being a Good Steward of Resources

Procurement isn't just about getting stuff; it's also about being responsible with what God has given us. By making smart choices about what we buy and how we use it, we're practicing good stewardship. This means getting the most value and making sure nothing goes to waste in our engineering adventures.

Key Takeaway

Procurement is the essential skill of wisely acquiring all the resources needed for your projects, ensuring quality, cost-effectiveness, and timely delivery. By mastering procurement, you become a responsible and efficient Creationeer, building your designs on a strong foundation while practicing good stewardship.
