Course Introduction

Concept Overview

Welcome to Creationeering!

Get ready to become a Junior Creationeer! This course isn't just about building; it's about learning a special way of thinking called Creationeering™. We'll use an 8-step process to design and optimize a mousetrap car, learning how everything in the world is connected.

Thinking Like an Engineer

In Creationeering, we'll focus on important mechanical engineering ideas. We'll learn about 'Multiscale Modeling,' which means understanding how tiny parts affect big things, and how to use 'Science as a Tool' to test our ideas about friction, energy, and power. Your engineering notebook will be super important for recording all your discoveries!

Learning from 'Findings'

Don't be afraid of mistakes! In Creationeering, we see 'flaws' as 'findings' that help us improve. We'll use 'Iteration' – a cycle of designing, testing, and making things better – to refine our mousetrap cars. It takes patience, but each test helps your design get stronger, just like gold is refined by fire.

Creationeering & Business

Beyond building, you'll also learn how to think like an entrepreneur! This course will guide you through the exciting steps of setting up and running a business, all the way to launching a product. Every Creationeer is also a steward of their creations, understanding how to manage and share their ideas with the world.

Key Takeaway

Creationeering is a powerful way to understand, design, and build, combining engineering, scientific thinking, and even business skills. You'll learn to see problems as opportunities, use science to find solutions, and refine your ideas through careful testing, all while developing a mindset of innovation and stewardship.
