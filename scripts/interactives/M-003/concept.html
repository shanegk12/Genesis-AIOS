Build Kit Overview

Concept Overview

Your Engineering Toolkit: What's Inside?

This section introduces the Mousetrap Car Kit as your hands-on learning tool for engineering. We'll explore all the parts you'll use to build your car, understanding why each piece is important for learning cool engineering ideas. Think of it as getting to know your building blocks!

Powering Your Ride: The Mousetrap Engine

The mousetrap isn't just for catching mice; it's your car's engine! We'll learn how its spring stores energy and then releases it to make your car move. You'll even get to figure out how much power your mousetrap has and how to use it best.

Building a Strong Foundation: Frame, Axles, and Transmission

Just like a house needs a strong foundation, your car needs a sturdy frame. We'll see how the balsa wood, lever arm, string, and brass axles work together to create the car's body and transfer power. Getting these parts just right is super important for your car to run smoothly!

Making it Move: Wheels and Traction

The wheels are where all that stored energy finally turns into movement! This section will explore how the size of your wheels and the grip of your balloons affect how far and how well your car travels. You'll discover how to make your car go the distance.

Key Takeaway

Every single part in your Mousetrap Car Kit is a mini-lesson in engineering. Understanding how each piece works and connects to others is key to building a successful car and mastering important engineering concepts. Get ready to design, build, and test!
