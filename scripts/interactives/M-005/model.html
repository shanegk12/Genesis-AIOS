Components

Chassis
Main structural frame. Transfers all forces between components. Baseline variable in Mark 1.0.
Rear Axle
Rotates to convert string tension into wheel motion. Key OFAT variable for distance optimization.
Wheels
4 wheels: rear drive wheels (large) and front steering wheels (small). Diameter affects speed vs. torque.
Lever Arm
Spring-loaded arm. Stores elastic potential energy. Extension length is a critical OFAT variable.
Drive String
Connects lever arm to rear axle. Transfers energy from spring to wheels. Length must match geometry.
