Build 1: Little Moe Prototype Car

Concept Overview

Welcome, Junior Creationeers!

Get ready to move from planning to building! We've spent time thinking, calculating, and defining our goals for our mousetrap car. Now, it's time to get our hands dirty and build a prototype to test all those awesome ideas.

Building Blocks: Subassemblies

Our Mousetrap Mark 1.0 car isn't built all at once. We'll break it down into smaller, manageable parts called subassemblies: the Chassis (the car's body), the Front Axle (for steering), and the Rear Axle (for power). Think of them as individual LEGO sets that come together to form one cool creation!

Mousetrap Makeover: Prepping the Chassis

Our first step is to transform a regular mousetrap into the core of our car's chassis. We'll carefully use pliers to remove certain parts, like pins and loops, to prepare it for its new role. This is like customizing a toy before you add all the cool accessories!

Assembling the Chassis: The Car's Foundation

Once our mousetrap is prepped, we'll start attaching other parts like the lever arm and balsa rails to create the sturdy base of our prototype car. This foundation is super important because it holds everything else together. The better we build it now, the easier the rest of the car will be!

Key Takeaway

Building a prototype helps us test our ideas in the real world. By breaking down a big project into smaller subassemblies and following steps carefully, we can create something awesome and learn a lot along the way!
