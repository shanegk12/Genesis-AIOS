Business Plan

Concept Overview

From Designer to Entrepreneur

You've designed and built an amazing mousetrap vehicle! Now, imagine taking that invention and turning it into a real product for others to enjoy. This lesson helps you think like an entrepreneur, someone who creates a business to bring new ideas to life.

Building Your Business Foundation

Launching a company isn't just about having a great idea; it's about planning how you'll make, market, and sell your product. You'll learn the essential steps to create a solid business plan, just like real companies do, to make sure your Creationeering™ Vehicle Company is ready for success.

Engineering Meets Enterprise

Every step of the Engineering Lifecycle, from brainstorming to testing, has business implications. You'll discover how decisions about design, materials, and production directly affect your company's budget, timeline, and ability to deliver a quality product to your customers.

Stewarding Your Creation

God has given us talents and resources, and this lesson teaches us to manage them wisely. You'll explore how to be a good steward of your company's money, materials, and the time you invest, ensuring your business honors God while serving others.

Key Takeaway

This lesson shows that engineering isn't just about building; it's also about smart planning, wise resource management, and understanding how to bring your creations to the world responsibly, reflecting God's design for order and purpose.
