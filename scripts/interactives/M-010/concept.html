Business Activity: Company Identit

Concept Overview

Building Your Business from the Ground Up

Just like a house needs a strong foundation, your mousetrap car company needs a clear identity! We'll start by giving your company a name and writing a mission statement. This statement is like your company's promise to your customers and your community, showing how you'll use your skills responsibly, just as Proverbs 16:3 teaches us to commit our work to God.

Who's in Charge? Your Team's Superpowers!

Every successful team has different roles, and your company is no different! We'll assign each team member a special leadership role, like a CEO or a Head Engineer. Each role is a 'pillar' supporting a different part of building your mousetrap car, ensuring every step of the process is covered and everyone knows their unique contribution.

Mapping Your Journey: From Idea to Awesome Car!

Now that you have your team, how will you actually get your car built? We'll create an organizational chart to show how everyone works together and a 'Critical Path' to map out all the steps, from designing to testing. This helps us see exactly what needs to happen and when, making sure we manage our time, talent, and materials wisely – that's called stewardship!

How Do We Know We're Winning?

It's great to build a car, but how will you know if your company is truly successful? We'll set three clear goals, or 'success metrics,' to measure your progress. These goals will help you understand if you've met your mission, delivered a great product, and used your resources responsibly.

Key Takeaway

Building a company is more than just making a product; it's about working together with purpose, managing your resources wisely, and serving your customers and community with integrity. By defining your identity, roles, and plan, you're learning essential business skills rooted in the principle of stewardship.
