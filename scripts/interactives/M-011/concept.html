Fundamentals of Design

Concept Overview

From Thinking to Doing: The Design Phase

We've explored how God designed the universe with amazing precision. Now, it's our turn to put that 'engineering thinking' into action! We're moving from just thinking about design to actually creating and building, starting with our mousetrap cars.

The Engineering Design Process: Your Blueprint for Success

Every great design starts with a plan. We'll learn to define our problem, set clear objectives, understand our limitations (constraints), and identify what we can change (variables). This OCV method helps us create a roadmap for designing, testing, and improving our mousetrap cars.

Prototyping and Optimization: Building Better Designs

Designing isn't just about making one thing; it's about making it the best it can be! We'll start with sketches or computer models, then build physical prototypes. By testing and making changes, we can optimize our designs, even using computers to quickly find the best solutions before building anything new.

Form, Function, and God's Orderly Design

Remember that 'form follows function'—how something looks is often determined by what it needs to do, just like Noah's Ark was shaped to survive the flood. Our designs should also reflect God's order and intentionality, showing care and discipline in how we create.

Key Takeaway

The 'Fundamentals of Design' teaches us to move from big ideas to practical creation. By following a clear design process, using prototypes, and learning from God's orderly creation, we can build and optimize amazing things, starting with our mousetrap cars!
