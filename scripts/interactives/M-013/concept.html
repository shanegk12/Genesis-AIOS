Build 2: Iterative Design - Mark 1

Concept Overview

Iterative Design: Your First Big Step

Welcome to Mark 1, where you'll start improving your design step-by-step. Your goal is to gather 'Ground Truth' data by carefully testing your car to understand how different parts affect its performance. This is the 'Analysis' part of being a Creationeer!

The 'One Factor At A Time' (OFAT) Rule

When you test, the most important rule is to change 'one and only one' thing between each set of trials. This helps you clearly see what impact that single change had, turning any unexpected results into valuable 'findings' for your design.

Your Testing Process: Control, Change, Test, Reset!

First, establish a 'Baseline Control' by testing your standard car multiple times and recording its average distance. Then, pick one variable, swap the part, run new tests, and record everything. Before testing the *next* variable, always switch back to your original control setup to keep your results clear.

What You'll Test and Why It Matters

You'll be testing variables like wheel size, axle type, and even wind angle. Each test helps you understand how forces like friction, drag, or torque affect your car's speed and distance, preparing you to make smart improvements for your Mark 2 design.

Key Takeaway

The Mark 1 phase teaches you that careful, 'one factor at a time' testing is essential to understand how each part of your design works. This systematic approach helps you gather crucial data to make informed decisions and build a much better Mark 2.
