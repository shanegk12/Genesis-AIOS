Build 2: Iterative Design - Mark21

Concept Overview

From Little Moe to Mark 2: Building on Success

Welcome back, Junior Creationeers! You've already built the Little Moe (Mark 1), learning how energy makes things move. Now, it's time to take all those lessons and combine them to build the Mark 2 – a super-improved version of your car. This isn't just a new car; it's a smarter car, built from everything you discovered.

The Symphony of Design: Combining Your Best Ideas

Remember how you tested one thing at a time, like different wheel types or string sizes? That was like practicing instruments separately. Now, we're bringing all those 'best' parts together like an orchestra. Every part needs to work perfectly with the others to make your Mark 2 go farther and faster.

Objectives, Constraints, and Variables: Your Blueprint for Mark 2

Before you even touch your materials, we need to plan. Think about your goals (Objectives), what limits you have (Constraints), and what parts you can change (Variables). Your Mark 1 data will help you make smart choices for your Mark 2, ensuring every decision is based on what you've already learned.

Precision Matters: Making Every Part Perfect

Your Mark 2 is like a body, and every part (or 'subassembly') needs to be perfect. For example, a heavy frame wastes energy, but a flimsy one will bend. You'll even polish your axles super smooth to reduce friction, making sure every tiny detail helps your car perform its best.

Key Takeaway

The Mark 2 project is all about 'Synthesis' – taking your individual discoveries from Mark 1 and combining them with precision to create a much better, optimized vehicle. Every choice you make, from the frame to the axles, should be based on your data and aimed at making your Mark 2 the best it can be.
