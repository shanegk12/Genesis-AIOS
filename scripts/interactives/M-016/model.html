Components

Chassis (Frame)
Balsa wood base. Must be rigid, lightweight, and symmetrical for straight travel.
Mousetrap (Spring)
Energy source. Stores potential energy in torsion spring; releases to drive lever arm.
Lever Arm
Extended arm attached to trap. Longer arm = more torque applied to drive axle string.
Rear Axle + Wheels
Brass rod axle with CD or balsa wheels. Smooth bearings reduce friction losses.
Front Axle + Wheels
Steerable or fixed. Alignment critical — even 1° of steering error causes drift.
Drive String
Connects lever arm tip to rear axle. Converts rotational PE into linear wheel motion.

OCV Framework

Objective
Maximize distance traveled from stored spring energy
Constraints
One standard mousetrap · Balsa + brass materials only · Must roll straight
Variables
Wheel diameter · Lever arm length · Axle bearing type · String attachment point
