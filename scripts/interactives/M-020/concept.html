Build 3: CAD Analysis of Design Parameters

Concept Overview

From Physical to Digital: Why We Need a Digital Twin

Imagine trying to build a perfect mousetrap car by hand, changing one tiny part at a time. It would take forever and use up tons of materials! A Digital Twin is like building your car in a computer program, allowing you to test thousands of ideas without wasting any wood or glue. This helps us be smart stewards of the resources God has given us.

Rapid Iteration: Speeding Up Your Design Process

In the past, you used OFAT and Synthesis to improve your physical cars. Now, with our Computational Mousetrap Car Model, you can make changes and see the results almost instantly. This 'Rapid Iteration' means you can discover the best possible designs much faster than ever before, just like the Master Creator who understands all the laws of physics perfectly.

Finding the Sweet Spot: Objective-Constraint-Variable (OCV) and the Pareto Frontier

Our digital model lets you play with different parts of your car using sliders for things like wheel size or lever length. You'll use these OCV sliders to predict how your car will perform. Your goal is to find the 'Pareto Frontier,' which is the perfect mix of all your variables that makes your car the most efficient it can be.

Understanding Energy: Being a Steward of the Joule

The simulation also helps you see how energy flows through your car, how much is lost, and how efficient your design is. By understanding these numbers, you learn to be a better 'Steward of the Joule,' meaning you're smart about how you use and convert energy, just as God designed the universe to be efficient and orderly.

Key Takeaway

Using a Digital Twin and computational modeling helps us design better mousetrap cars much faster and without wasting resources. It teaches us to think like engineers, using God's predictable laws of physics to find the most efficient and effective solutions.
