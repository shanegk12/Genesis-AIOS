📚 Key Vocabulary Review

Procurement — Obtaining and making the materials needed for a design.
Raw Material — Basic unprocessed material used to make something.
Component — A pre-manufactured part of a larger whole.
Supplier — A person or company that provides materials.
Quality Control — Ensuring products meet specific standards.
