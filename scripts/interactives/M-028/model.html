Bill of Materials

Chassis
Wooden base frame that supports all components. Provides structural rigidity.
⬜ 30cm × 10cm balsa/basswood
Mousetrap
Spring-loaded energy source. The arm releases stored mechanical energy to drive the string.
✓ Standard Victor trap · verify spring tension
Rear Axle
Transfers rotational force from the string to the rear wheels. Must be straight and smooth.
⬜ 3mm wooden dowel · check for warping
Wheels (×4)
CD/wheel discs that minimize friction and maximize rolling distance. Check for roundness.
⬜ 12cm dia · verify center hole alignment
Drive String
Connects the mousetrap arm to the rear axle. Must be low-stretch for reliable energy transfer.
⬜ 60cm nylon string · check for fraying
Lever Extension
Extends the mousetrap arm to increase mechanical advantage and travel distance.
⬜ 30cm dowel extension · verify angle
