Mousetrap Build
Components
Chassis
The main frame of the car. Supports all other components and provides structural integrity during launch.
Mousetrap Spring
Stores potential energy when set. When released, it snaps the lever arm forward, converting stored energy to kinetic energy.
Lever Arm
A rigid bar that pivots on the mousetrap fulcrum. Extends the snap distance and amplifies torque transferred to the string.
Axle
A rod passing through wheel centers. Converts the string's pull into rotation. Reduces friction when smooth and properly aligned.
Wheels
Turn on the axle to propel the car. Larger wheels travel farther per rotation; wider wheels improve traction.
Drive String
Connects the lever arm to the rear axle. As the arm snaps, the string unwinds from the axle, spinning the wheels.
