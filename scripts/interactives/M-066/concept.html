BA: Business Plan Compilation + Pitch

Concept Overview

Your Big Idea: From Problem to Product

Every great business starts with solving a problem! Think about something that's difficult or missing, then imagine how your mousetrap car kit can make it better. This is where you describe your awesome product and what makes it special for your customers.

Who Are You Selling To? Your Target Audience

Not everyone will buy your product, and that's okay! It's super important to know exactly who your ideal customer is. This section helps you focus on the people who will love your mousetrap car kit the most, like specific types of families or schools.

Show Me the Money! Pricing and Breaking Even

Running a business means understanding money. You need to know how much to charge for your mousetrap car kit and how many you need to sell just to cover your costs. This 'break-even' point helps you see if your business idea can actually make a profit.

Your Winning Edge: Why Choose You?

There might be other products out there, so what makes yours the best? This is your 'competitive advantage' – what makes your mousetrap car kit stand out. Also, think about how your business can be good for the planet with a 'sustainability plan'.

Key Takeaway

Putting together a business plan and practicing your pitch helps you clearly explain your ideas, understand your market, and prepare for the real world of entrepreneurship. It's all about planning smart so you can execute well!
