These three elements define every optimization problem you will ever face as an engineer:</p>\n<ul>\n  <li><strong>Objective:</strong> What do you want to achieve? This is your goal — maximize speed, minimize cost, increase efficiency.</li>\n  <li><strong>Constraint:</strong> What limits do you have to work within? Budget, size, weight, safety requirements — these are your boundaries.</li>\n  <li><strong>Variable:</strong> What can you actually change? These are the knobs you can turn — material choice, shape, size, motor power.</li>\n</ul>\n<p>By clearly defining all three, you can make systematic, data-driven decisions instead of randomly trying things and hoping for the best. It's the difference between a scientist and a guesser.</p>"
